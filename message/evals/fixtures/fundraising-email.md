We have been working to pass legislation that would provide state funding to keep rural hospitals open, but the bill has stalled in committee for the third year in a row.

There is a lot of work still to do, and we can't do it without support from people like you. Every dollar helps us cover the cost of research, outreach, and advocacy so we can keep pushing this issue forward.

Discrimination against LGBTQ people continues to be a serious problem in Texas. Every year, gay and transgender Texans are denied housing, fired from jobs, and turned away from public places because of who they are. It is unfortunate that these harms are still allowed to happen under current law. We are against discrimination in all its forms and believe it should not be tolerated. It may be the case that stronger protections could help address some of these issues over time.

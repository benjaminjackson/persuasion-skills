Subject: Every family deserves a home they can count on

Neighbor,

Every family in this town deserves a home they can count on, at a price they can actually afford. That's the plain idea behind everything we're doing this spring.

But a small group of corporate landlords bought up half the rental housing on the east side over the past two years, and they've raised the rent on every single unit since. Families who've lived here for a decade are getting priced out of their own neighborhood, and the landlords are choosing to do this even as they report record profits to their investors.

We're bringing a rent stabilization measure to the city council next month, and we need your name on the petition to get it on the agenda.

Sign the petition today, and let's make sure every family here has a home they can count on.

— The East Side Tenants Council
